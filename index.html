Heritage & Values

Welcome to the LSA

“Discipline, Perfection and Success”
– A temple for the students

We are dedicated to providing high-quality education to students of all ages. Our curriculum balances academic learning with sports, debates, dramatics, and Atal Tinkering Labs (ATL) to build intellectually curious and morally conscious leaders.

Our supportive, challenging environment offers the perfect foundation for scholars to thrive, develop character values (respect, honesty, and kindness), and discover their true potentials.

Campus Facilities

Providing modern resources to assist scholars in both academic and creative endeavors.

Co-Curricular Activities

(a) Sports & Games: Active physical tournaments and sports coaching.

(b) Cultural Arts: Group Dance, classical Music & Dramatics studies.

(c) Competitions: Essay writing contests, Quiz leagues, and Debates.

(d) Crafting: Chart making and creative Scrapbook compilation.

(e) Annual Days: Grand Annual Functions and Sports Meet.

Infrastructure & Tools

(a) Transport Fleet: Secure bus routing covering all neighboring regions.

(b) Academic Library: Well-stocked libraries with classic storybooks.

(c) Computer Center: Modern desktop systems to instruct computer science basics from Grade 1.

(d) Audio-Visual Labs: Modern interactive panels used as teaching tools.

Parent-Teacher Meets

Exam Reviews: PTM consultancies scheduled regularly after terminal exams to track progress.

Consultation: Meet class teachers on the first Saturday of every month to check performance cards.

Syllabus Guides

Printed course blueprints outlining examinations, textbooks, holidays, and event details are distributed.

Academic Session

The academic session starts in April and ends in mid-May of the following year, split into standard grading terms.

Admission Testing

Assessments evaluate proficiency in English, Hindi, and Science, matching preceding class standards.
